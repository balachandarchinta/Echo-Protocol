# Echo Protocol Development Roadmap

# AI-Powered Crime Intelligence & Predictive Analytics Platform

---

# 1. Vision

Echo Protocol aims to become a comprehensive AI-powered Crime Intelligence Platform that enables law enforcement agencies to transform operational crime data into actionable intelligence through analytics, machine learning, and intelligent decision support.

The roadmap follows an incremental delivery approach, where each phase builds upon the previous one while maintaining a scalable and modular architecture.

# 13. Conclusion

Echo Protocol has successfully completed its foundational phase by delivering a cloud-native, AI-enabled Crime Intelligence Platform on Zoho Catalyst. The platform now includes centralized crime management, operational analytics, interactive dashboards, and a production-ready QuickML-based Crime Severity Prediction model.

The roadmap focuses on progressively expanding AI Intelligence, strengthening security and governance, enhancing predictive capabilities, and evolving Echo Protocol into a comprehensive Smart Policing platform capable of supporting modern law enforcement through data-driven and AI-assisted decision making.
